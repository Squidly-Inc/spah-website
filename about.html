Julie Sullivan

Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Julie Sullivan, the founder of SPAH, Spa Parties at Home, and I started this business back in 2010 out of my love for crafts and working with kids. As a teacher, I've always believed in the power of creativity, imagination, and making every child feel included and celebrated.

Over the years, I've built an amazing team of women who help bring our parties to life, but I still personally love being part of the creative process—especially when it comes to our spa-inspired crafts.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

What Makes SPAH Different

✦

Creativity First

Our focus is never on appearance. We create a welcoming space where kids can express themselves and get creative.

♥

Real Connections

Parties are about laughter, friendship, and making meaningful memories — not just activities.

☆

Stress-Free for Parents

We bring everything, set up, run the party, and clean up. All you do is relax and enjoy.

♛

Every Child Included

Every guest gets the full experience — robes, treatments, crafts, and a favor bag to take home.

THE SPAH CREW

Meet the Team

Behind every party is an amazing team of talented women who bring the experience to life.

Creative, Warm & Wonderful

Each member of our team is not only warm, fun, and wonderful with children, but also truly creative at heart — artists when it comes to hair, nails, and all things spa-inspired. Some of whom even had their own SPAH parties when they were younger ;)

They love transforming each guest with stylish updos, polished nails, and a touch of sparkle, while also guiding the kids through our hands-on spa crafts.

Making Every Party Special

Their energy, creativity, and genuine joy make every party feel special, helping create a fun, welcoming atmosphere where kids can relax, express themselves, and feel pampered from start to finish.
