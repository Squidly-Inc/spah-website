Our Story

Meet Julie.

Hi, I'm Julie Sullivan, the founder of SPAH, Spa Parties at Home, and I started this business back in 2010 out of my love for crafts and working with kids. As a teacher, I've always believed in the power of creativity, imagination, and making every child feel included and celebrated.

Over the years, I've built an amazing team of women who help bring our parties to life, but I still personally love being part of the creative process—especially when it comes to our spa-inspired crafts.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

It's About More Than Spa Days

While we offer spa-themed activities, our focus is never on appearance. Instead, we create a fun, welcoming space where kids can express themselves, get creative, and simply enjoy being together.

To us, parties are about more than just activities—they're about laughter, friendship, and making meaningful memories. Our goal is to give every child a joyful, creative experience while making the day easy and stress-free for parents.
